Some older diffs are empty or abbreviated and PDF / Finder metadata blobs are not text-scannable. **Do not treat a negative pattern scan as proof that Git history contains no secrets.** A local history-aware scanner (e.g. Gitleaks) plus a binary/metadata inspection should be run before declaring a clean bill of health.

## Permitted / prohibited work at Stage 01

Any content release or removal decision needs separate approval and validation.
